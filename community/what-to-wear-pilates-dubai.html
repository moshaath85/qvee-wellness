What to wear to a Pilates class in Dubai — and what to bring

Practical 21 July 2026 3 min read

Pilates is one of the few practices where what you wear genuinely affects how well you move. Here is what works, what to avoid, and what you can leave at home.

Clothing

Choose fitted, flexible activewear that stays close to the body. On the reformer, loose trousers catch under the carriage and baggy tops bunch around your shoulders — both make it harder for your instructor to see your alignment.

  • Leggings or fitted shorts — full length is comfortable for reformer work.
  • A fitted top or sports bra — nothing that rides up when you raise your arms.
  • Bare feet with grip socks — no trainers on the reformer or the mat.

Dubai studios are cool inside; a light layer is useful before and after class, especially in summer.

Grip socks really do matter

Grip socks are the one requirement we hold firmly. Bare feet slip on the carriage and the mat, and slipping mid-exercise is how strain happens. Socks with rubberised soles give you purchase, keep the equipment hygienic, and make footwork feel far more controlled.

Bring your own, or buy a pair at reception — we keep several sizes.

Hair, jewellery and hydration

Tie long hair back so it does not catch on the carriage or distract you during roll-downs. Remove large earrings and necklaces, and cut rings if they dig into the reformer straps. Bring a water bottle; reformer classes look gentle but they are a proper workout.

What the studio provides

At QVee Wellness everything needed for the class itself is waiting for you: the reformer, mats, props, resistance bands, rings and cleaning supplies. There is nothing to carry in, and nothing to clean down afterwards — that is what your class fee includes.

A short packing list

  • Fitted top and leggings
  • Grip socks
  • Water bottle
  • Hair tie
  • Optional light layer for before and after

That is genuinely all. The rest — the calm, the equipment and the quiet luxury of the room — is already here.

Forgot your socks? Ask at reception before your class starts. We would always rather give you a pair than have you slip through a session on bare feet.
