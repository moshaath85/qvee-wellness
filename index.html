About Us

A new standard for women's wellness

QVee Wellness is a luxury women-only wellness destination located in the heart of Sobha Hartland, Dubai. Designed to redefine modern wellness, it blends movement, mindfulness, recovery and community within an elegant, hospitality-inspired environment.

Our studio offers premium Pilates, yoga, wellness programmes and transformative experiences — designed to support women in achieving physical strength, mental clarity and overall wellbeing.

Our Story

Founder's Message

"QVee Wellness was born from a simple belief — that women deserve a space designed entirely for them, where strength is nurtured with grace and wellbeing is treated as a way of life."

We created a sanctuary that honours the modern woman — her ambition, her balance, and her need for a place that feels both elevated and deeply personal. This is more than a studio; it is a home for transformation.

Qamar Ibrahim Founder & Managing Director
