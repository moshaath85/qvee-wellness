The Details

Good to know before you arrive

Everything is considered, so nothing is left to wonder. Should you need anything at all, our team is always on hand.

All sessions are reserved in advance to preserve the intimacy of every class. Book online, or reach us directly and we will arrange it for you.

Please arrive fifteen minutes before your session. Grip socks are required for reformer classes; mats, props and everything else are provided within the studio.

Convenient parking is available within One Park Avenue, Sobha Hartland. Our team will gladly guide you on arrival.

The studio may be reserved exclusively for private groups, celebrations and corporate wellbeing days. Speak with us to tailor the experience.
