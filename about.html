Our Story

Welcome to QVee Wellness

QVee Wellness is a luxury women-only wellness destination located in the heart of Sobha Hartland, Dubai. Designed to redefine modern wellness, it blends movement, mindfulness, recovery and community within an elegant, hospitality-inspired environment.

Our studio offers premium Pilates, yoga, wellness programmes and transformative experiences — created to support women in achieving physical strength, mental clarity and overall wellbeing. Every detail, from the soft daylight to the natural textures, has been considered to make each visit feel exceptional.

The result is a space that feels closer to a private members' retreat than a studio: calm, refined and entirely devoted to women.

Founder's Message

"QVee Wellness was born from a simple belief — that women deserve a space designed entirely for them, where strength is nurtured with grace and wellbeing is treated as a way of life."

We created a sanctuary that honours the modern woman — her ambition, her balance, and her need for a place that feels both elevated and deeply personal. This is more than a studio; it is a home for transformation. Every class, every corner and every welcome has been shaped around that intention.
