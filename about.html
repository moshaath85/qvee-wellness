QVee Wellness

Good movement.
Personal attention.

QVee is a women-only Pilates and wellness studio founded by Qamar Ibrahim in Sobha Hartland, Dubai.

Our classes range from small-group Reformer Pilates and mat work to yoga and one-to-one training. Instructors guide each session, with room to ask questions and move at your own pace.

The studio brings together a Reformer space, yoga and mat areas, a private training room and a reception lounge.
