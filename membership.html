The finer details

Before you choose.

Everything you need to choose your next step.

What is the difference between a package and a membership?

Class packages offer a set number of classes, with validity confirmed for your selected plan. Monthly memberships cover 30 days. Choose Reformer Pilates, Mat Pilates, Yoga or Aerial Yoga and see the included classes and terms before enquiring.

What does Unlimited Reformer include?

Unlimited Reformer Pilates for 30 days, one Guest Pass and free access to Mat Pilates. AED 3,360 + VAT per month. Booking and monthly membership terms apply.

Can I pause my monthly membership?

Yes. All monthly memberships can be frozen for up to 60 days. Contact the studio to arrange your freeze.

Which memberships include a Guest Pass?

The 12-visit and 20-visit Reformer monthly memberships include a Guest Pass. Contact the studio for guest booking details.

Is Mat Pilates included?

The 12-visit and 20-visit Reformer monthly memberships also include access to Mat Pilates. Contact the studio for scheduling and access details.

How do I purchase or book?

Our Mindbody booking connection is coming soon. For now, enquire about your preferred plan by email. An enquiry does not confirm a purchase or reserve a class.
