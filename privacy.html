Privacy at QVee.

Last updated: 14 October 2026.

Studio enquiries

When you send an enquiry, we receive your name, email, selected interest and message, plus your phone number if you choose to provide it. We use these details to respond to your request and discuss our services.

How enquiries are handled

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Currently, the enquiry form prepares a draft in your email application. Your message is sent only when you choose to send that email, and is then handled by our email provider. Online submission through Netlify Forms is prepared but is not currently enabled. Please do not submit payment details, medical information or other sensitive documents through this form.

Booking and payment

Mindbody booking is not yet active on this website. We do not currently collect payment card details or create customer booking accounts here. Information about the booking provider will be updated when that service is enabled.

Site storage

The site may store public page and image files in your browser to improve repeat visits and provide offline access. Form submissions are not saved in this public page cache. Our hosting provider may process technical connection information to operate and secure the site.

Contact us

For questions about your information or to request access, correction or deletion, email contact@qveewellness.com.
